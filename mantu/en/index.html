A-01 Section 1-1Services

One building. Every trade.

Pick a job in the list or a part of the building in the drawing. You can order the whole build or a single stage.

  • The whole build in one company: from foundations to the last finishing detail.

  • Foundations, floor slabs and masonry. The base the whole house stands on.

  • Roof structures, covering and flashing for new houses and reconstruction.

  • Facade work for new and renovated buildings.

  • Plastering, filling, painting, floors, tiles and doors.

  • Building services: electrical installation, water supply, drainage and heating.

  • Timber-frame houses, extensions and terraces.

  • Renewal of old buildings: from structure to finishing.
