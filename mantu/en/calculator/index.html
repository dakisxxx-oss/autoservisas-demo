      How we calculate

      We calculate from the bottom up: from the floor area and rooms we get the wall, ceiling and floor areas, and from them the quantities of work.

      For each job we apply a market rate and the price of the chosen materials, with allowances for waste.

      We add a contingency for unexpected work and show a range, because quantities and prices are never exact in advance.

      Price sources and date: November 2026, Lithuanian market. Every line has a source and a reliability mark in the table below.

      How to read the result

      The “from ... to” range shows the likely limits. “About” is the medium scenario. The more answers you refine, the narrower the range.

      This is a preliminary price, not an offer. We will discuss the exact price when we get in touch.
